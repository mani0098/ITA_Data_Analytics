# Sprint 08 — Power BI: Sales Analysis

## 📌 Overview

This sprint focuses on **advanced data visualization and business intelligence using Power BI**, emphasizing exploratory analysis, interactive reporting, and effective communication of insights.

The practical assignment involves analyzing **five years of sales data**, identifying trends, patterns, and anomalies, and developing interactive dashboards to investigate business performance and support data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Select appropriate **data visualizations** based on variable types and analytical questions.
- Design **interactive Power BI reports** with clear and effective visual storytelling.
- Apply **data filtering and transformation** techniques to enhance analysis.
- Strengthen the use of **DAX** for advanced calculations and business metrics.
- Identify meaningful **trends, seasonality, and anomalies** in sales data.
- Apply visualization and report-design best practices to improve usability and communication.

## 💻 Practical Assignment

The exercises are divided into three progressive levels:

| Level | Focus | Key Tasks |
|:---|:---|:---|
| ⭐ Level 1 | Exploratory Data Analysis | Formulate 3–5 business questions, build appropriate visualizations, and interpret findings through a concise dashboard. |
| ⭐⭐ Level 2 | Sales Trends & Pattern Recognition | Analyze five-year sales trends, explore business segments, identify seasonal patterns, and investigate unusual sales behavior. |
| ⭐⭐⭐&nbsp;Level&nbsp;3 | Business Performance Investigation | Investigate the sales decline in 2023–2024, compare historical performance, formulate hypotheses, and present findings through an interactive report. |

## 🛠️ Technologies & Skills

**Tools:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop, Power Query

**Concepts:** Exploratory Data Analysis · DAX · Interactive Dashboards · Time-Series Analysis · Trend & Seasonality Detection · Data Storytelling · Business Intelligence
