# Sprint 07 — Introduction to Data Analysis with Power BI and Indicators

## 📌 Overview

This sprint introduces **Microsoft Power BI** as a business intelligence tool, focusing on data transformation, exploratory analysis, and developing **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)** using DAX.

The practical assignment involves analyzing an **online sales and transaction database**, creating interactive reports to monitor business performance, identify trends, and support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Understand the **Power BI interface** and its data modeling capabilities.
- Import, clean, and transform data using **Power Query**.
- Create calculated columns and measures using **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)**.
- Develop **KPIs** to evaluate business performance against defined targets.
- Apply descriptive statistics and design effective analytical visualizations.
- Communicate business insights through interactive Power BI reports.

## 💻 Practical Assignment

The exercises are divided into three progressive levels:

| Level | Focus | Key Tasks |
|:---|:---|:---|
| ⭐ Level 1 | Data Exploration & KPI Development | Analyze annual and monthly sales, create performance indicators, evaluate company distribution by country, and examine user transactions with currency conversions. |
| ⭐⭐ Level 2 | Sales Trends & Performance Analysis | Analyze monthly sales trends in Germany (2018–2019), measure performance against sales targets, and evaluate average transaction values using DAX measures. |
| ⭐⭐⭐&nbsp;Level&nbsp;3 | Customer & Product Analytics | Integrate customer and product data, calculate purchase statistics, analyze customer spending, identify product price extremes, and visualize geographic customer distribution. |

## 🛠️ Technologies & Skills

**Tools:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop, Power Query

**Concepts:** Data Transformation · Data Modeling · DAX · KPI Development · Descriptive Statistics · Sales Analytics · Customer Segmentation · Interactive Dashboards
